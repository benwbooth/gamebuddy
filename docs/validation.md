## Automatic conversation audio ducking

The Rust/PipeWire fixture measured game output at **0.251 of its original amplitude (−12 dB)** on a private virtual sink, then measured normal output after conversation silence. The full production Gamescope/WebRTC fixture passed under `/tmp/gamebuddy-realtime-test-Pxe6tgxt/`: the output-only spoken introduction ducked the fixture game's stream without microphone activation, quiet play restored it, an unmuted silent microphone left it unchanged, and synthetic microphone speech triggered ducking.
